TACTICAL BRIEFING

COMBAT DIRECTIVES • READ BEFORE ENGAGEMENT

▲ HOSTILE THREATS

Enemy words descend from above. Each word is a hostile unit that must be neutralized before breaching the defense perimeter at the bottom of the screen.

⚫ TURRET TARGETING

Type characters to fire your cannon. The turret auto-locks onto the lowest word starting with that character. Once locked, continue typing to eliminate the target.

● BALLISTIC FIRE

Each correct keystroke fires a visible projectile bullet. Typed characters fade to 38% opacity while remaining letters stay bright. A glowing cursor tracks your position.

★ RED BONUS TARGETS

Crimson targets fall faster and grant 3.5x score multiplier. While a red word falls, conflicting words are suppressed. A cooldown follows its destruction.

■ HULL INTEGRITY

Words breaching the perimeter detonate and damage your tank. Normal breach: -20% hull. Bonus breach: -30% hull. Tank destruction ends the sortie immediately.

⇈ SCORING & COMBO

Each elimination increases your combo multiplier. Missed keystrokes reset it. Red targets: 350 base pts x combo. Normal: 100 base x combo. Difficulty scales over time.

⚓ CONTROLS

Type to fire. ESC to abort sortie. Hull color: green > 50%, amber 25-50%, red < 25%. Check header for CRT, audio & ratio toggles.

⚖ RECORDS

All sorties are logged locally. New personal bests trigger an arcade celebration. Access your flight log from the result screen with [R] or the records button.
